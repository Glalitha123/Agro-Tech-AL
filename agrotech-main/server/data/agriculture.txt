# AgroTech Overview

AgroTech is a technology-driven agricultural support platform designed to provide farmers and users with information about crops, farming practices, market prices, weather conditions, pest management, and agricultural policies.

# Agricultural Practices

Agriculture involves soil preparation, seed selection, sowing, irrigation, fertilizer application, crop monitoring, pest control, harvesting, storage, transportation, and marketing.

# Agricultural Seasons

Common agricultural seasons in India include Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid. Crop selection and sowing periods depend on geographic location, rainfall, temperature, and weather conditions.

# Soil Management

Soil testing helps farmers understand soil fertility, pH levels, nutrient availability, and crop suitability. Proper soil management includes crop rotation, organic manure, composting, and erosion prevention.

# Seed Selection and Sowing

Farmers should select healthy and high-quality seeds suitable for local soil and climate conditions. Seed quality, planting depth, spacing, and sowing time influence crop productivity.

# Irrigation Management

Irrigation methods include surface irrigation, sprinkler irrigation, and drip irrigation. Farmers should select irrigation methods based on crop requirements, soil conditions, and water availability.

# Fertilizers and Nutrients

Fertilizers provide essential nutrients such as nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium. Fertilizer recommendations should consider soil test results, crop type, and crop growth stage.

# Pest and Disease Management

Farmers should correctly identify pests and crop diseases before applying pesticides, insecticides, or fungicides. Integrated Pest Management combines biological, cultural, mechanical, and chemical methods.

# Organic Farming

Organic farming includes composting, crop rotation, green manure, biological pest control, and reduced use of synthetic agricultural chemicals.

# Weather and Crop Advisory

Weather conditions such as rainfall, temperature, humidity, drought, floods, and extreme weather events can affect agricultural activities and crop productivity.

# Harvesting and Storage

Harvesting should be performed at the appropriate crop maturity stage. Post-harvest management includes cleaning, grading, drying, packaging, storage, and transportation.

# Agricultural Market Prices

Agricultural commodity prices may vary between states, districts, markets, mandis, seasons, and time periods based on demand, supply, quality, and transportation costs.

# Minimum Support Price MSP

Minimum Support Price is a government-announced price mechanism applicable to selected agricultural commodities. MSP information should be verified using current official government sources.

# Government Schemes and Subsidies

Agricultural schemes, subsidies, insurance programs, loans, and financial assistance are subject to eligibility requirements, documentation, deadlines, and government policy changes.

# Crop Insurance

Crop insurance coverage, premiums, claims, compensation, and eligibility conditions depend on the applicable insurance scheme and policy terms.

# Agricultural Loans

Agricultural loans may involve interest rates, repayment conditions, collateral requirements, and eligibility criteria determined by banks and financial institutions.

# AgroTech AI Assistant

The AgroTech AI assistant provides agricultural information based on user queries and available knowledge. Users should provide crop name, location, crop stage, symptoms, and soil conditions for more relevant responses.

# Vector Search and Pinecone

Agricultural documents are divided into smaller chunks and converted into vector embeddings using embedding models. The embeddings are stored and indexed in Pinecone for semantic search.

# User Queries

Users can ask questions about crops, soil, fertilizers, irrigation, pests, diseases, weather, market prices, MSP, government schemes, agricultural loans, and farming practices.

# Terms and Conditions

AgroTech information is provided for general informational and educational purposes. Users should verify important agricultural, financial, pesticide, fertilizer, and government policy decisions with qualified professionals or official sources.

# Disclaimer

AgroTech does not guarantee crop yield, market prices, weather accuracy, financial returns, government benefits, or the success of recommended agricultural practices. Agricultural information and policies may change over time.
